## Discover Studio

Use Studio to explore a GPS outing in your browser, edit its appearance on the map and turn it into images, a video or a report. A **journey** holds the whole outing. Its **tracks** draw the lines you see on the map. [Understand journeys and tracks](/user-guide/workflows/journeys-and-tracks/).

<span id="typical-session"></span>

## Create my first snapshot

Use a GPS file from your own outing, or choose the **LGS1920** sample journey to explore La Grande Sure. The quick guide then takes you through finding the track, framing the map and saving your first snapshot.

**[Follow the quick guide](/user-guide/getting-started/first-steps/)**
