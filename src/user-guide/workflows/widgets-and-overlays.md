
## Choose Widgets

Widgets are visual overlays rendered above the scene. Use them when the output needs context that the map alone cannot provide.

Common widgets include:

- Compass.
- Elevation profile.
- Journey statistics.
- Dynamic journey statistics for replay-driven values.
- Text.
- Credits.
- Logo.
- Scene-specific controls or visual elements.

## Place Widgets

Place widgets where they support the story and do not hide important route details. Check the same layout at the final capture size, not only during editing.

The widget management drawer controls board membership and ordering. The widget editor controls the selected widget's appearance and configuration. Use the grid and snap controls to align overlays, then check their position again after changing the video crop: widgets that are not editable remain anchored to the composition while the crop is resized.

## Export Ready Checks

Before capturing:

1. Keep important text inside the final capture area.
2. Avoid placing widgets over dense map details.
3. Check the visual order when overlays overlap.
4. Hide widgets that are only useful while editing.
5. Test the layout in the snapshot or video workflow before final export.
6. Keep the mandatory Logo and Credits widgets visible when the output requires branding or attribution.
7. Check dynamic statistics during replay; their visibility and values can change with replay progress.
