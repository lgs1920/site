
## Choose Scene Mode

Use the scene mode that best supports the output:

- 2D for flat map readability.
- 3D for terrain, depth, and route views with more spatial context.

## Focus The Target

Focus moves the camera to the selected object or coordinate. Use it before detailed framing, because it gives you a reliable starting point.

Recommended order:

1. Select the journey, track, POI, or coordinate.
2. Focus the target.
3. Adjust distance, heading, pitch, or height.
4. Check labels, markers, and widgets after the camera settles.

## Movement Controls

Orbit keeps the camera moving around a target. Panorama creates a moving viewpoint with adjustable height, pitch, speed, and direction.

Before recording:

1. Start the movement once.
2. Watch a full pass.
3. Stop movement and fix any framing issue.
4. Restart movement only when the scene, widgets, and capture area are ready.
