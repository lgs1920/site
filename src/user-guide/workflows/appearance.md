## Open Settings

Start from the Studio scene:

1. Click the `Settings` button.
2. The [Settings drawer](/user-guide/reference/studio-interface/drawers/settings/) opens.
3. Open the `User Interface` tab.

## Change Theme

Use theme mode when the workspace is too bright, too dark, or needs to follow the operating system.

1. Choose `Light`, `Dark`, or `System`.
2. Check the scene, drawers, and dialogs after the change.
3. Keep the mode that makes route lines, POIs, and controls easiest to read.

## Change Brand

Use brand and seasonal color settings when the Studio needs a different accent color.

1. Choose the brand color.
2. Choose the seasonal palette if the setting is available.
3. Check icon buttons, selected states, links, and table chips.
4. Avoid a combination where text, map labels, or controls lose contrast.

## Checks

Before capture or recording:

1. Check the route line and POIs against the map layer.
2. Check widgets against the scene background.
3. Check dialogs and drawers in the selected theme.
4. Keep the color setup stable before final export.
