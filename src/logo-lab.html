Concepts

Three square directions, built to stay readable in the header and on product surfaces.

01

Trail Play

Trek path, ridge, and a small play tile in one compact square.
02

Summit Screen

Mountain inside a screen frame, with the video cue embedded in the scene.
03

Peak Capture

More iconic, with a mountain block, a trekking line, and a vertical video module.

Active

The header now uses the Trail Play mark with the inline lockup.

LGS1920 Studio

It holds up best at smaller sizes and keeps the route-plus-output idea readable without stretching horizontally.

Notes

The main constraint was to stay compact while keeping three signals: map, movement, and output.

Square first

All three marks work as avatars, app icons, or favicon source material before the name is added.

Two lockups

The same mark supports a stacked version for social or splash surfaces and a right-side version for headers.

Product fit

The forms lean into routing, terrain, and capture instead of looking like a generic outdoor badge.
